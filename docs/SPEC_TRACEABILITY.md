## Scenario ownership

Shared fixture conventions first land in phase 11; shared verification lifecycle in phase 10. Later scenario phases consume those contracts and introduce their own fixture/stage requirements. Their specs do not replace the shared guardrails.

| Scenario | Implementation phase | Additional evidence beyond five fresh science windows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 First Shift | 11; model trials in 12 | 150 fresh gears and 150 copper plates from terminals into the chain |
| S2 Some Assembly Required | 13 | 300 iron/150 copper plate intake; 150 machine-made gears delivered to science |
| S3 Hot Metal | 14 | 300 iron/150 copper ore intake and smelted plate routing; gear chain; quantitative furnace fuel |
| S4 You Are the Infrastructure | 15 | Designated-patch extraction; full chain; bootstrap reserve isolation and mined-coal replenishment of power/smelting branches |
| S5 Unscheduled Downtime | 16 | S4 output/stage/fuel rules plus admission within ten game minutes and every-start unrepaired rejection |

Quantities above reflect the ordinary recipes documented in SCENARIOS and must be re-derived from the installed game. Common output remains at least 30 newly machine-produced and automatically collected packs in each of five consecutive 3600-tick windows. Upstream totals cover the same 18000 scored ticks. Proposed settling is 600 ticks, fixed before the run.

## Scope and design assumptions

The initial contracts accommodate surfaces, quality, fluids and probabilistic recipes but return explicit unsupported results for unimplemented advanced calculations. Combat, fresh-map survival, rockets/planetary expansion, cloud hosting, paid API access, screenshot-dependent gameplay, redistributed game assets and automatic blueprint libraries remain deferred.

Node 24/TypeScript/pnpm, Lua, local Codex app-server/MCP, Fastify/React/Vite and SQLite are inherited proposed choices to validate, not newly verified compatibility claims. Exact driver/protocol/game versions, hosting behavior, measurement coverage and upstream notices are resolved by named feasibility tasks. The [phase organization decision](decisions/002-openspec-phase-plan.md) adds no product-scope exception.
