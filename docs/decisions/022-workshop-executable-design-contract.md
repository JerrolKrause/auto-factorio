# Decision 022: executable workshop design contract

The workshop supplies unlimited allowed construction items and continuously replenished inputs. Item interfaces are actual protected express-belt tiles, with both lanes available; they are not chests labeled as belts. Brief-derived item sources supply 45 items per game second subject to physical backpressure. Burner fuel is a separate source, derived from installed machine energy and fuel facts. Targets requiring more than a port's physical capacity fail before inference. Fluid interfaces remain tanks; the power fixture supplies unlimited electricity but the design must distribute it with poles.

Installed machine speed, energy, fuel and equipment geometry accompany the assignment and participate in its compatibility fingerprint. The declared 500 by 500 design envelope includes the ports. Astra is selected by exact model ID, without fallback to whichever catalog entry comes first. The default objective is 15 iron plates per second with five game minutes of settling, then five one-minute measurement windows.

Preset revision 2 selects production machines while making yellow, red and blue transport equipment available with matching logistics research. The former yellow-only restriction imposed a 15/s input bottleneck even on advanced presets, defeating the source's headroom. Explicit custom profiles still control their own equipment. Designer instructions require capacity above the target across input trunks, individual lanes and output trunks.

The designer may return bounded declarative `entityGroups` with a repeat count, translation step and entity templates. The trusted host expands at most 10,000 entities, reserves explicit IDs and native numbers before assigning unused identities, and applies the same blueprint validation and game placement as explicit entities. Native wires keep their explicit endpoints. No model-authored code is executed. This keeps repeated placement arithmetic deterministic and avoids spending most of a model turn enumerating belts.

Each next design receives the previous candidate, evaluation and machine diagnostics. An acknowledged model-output rejection or atomically rolled-back direct build consumes an attempt and becomes feedback; it does not fabricate a measurement. Unknown provider/game outcomes still hold for exact reconciliation. Exhausted rejected attempts finish with no valid result. Conveyor contents and inserter-held items remain inside the stock-accounting boundary.

Model observations put immutable evaluation and score before geometry. The scorer receives entity counts and grouped diagnostic samples instead of thousands of repeated entities; counts and sampling markers expose the summary's limits. Full diagnostics remain in run evidence. Designer retries receive the original compact design representation, with canonical-document fallback for older artifacts. This prevents tool-response truncation from hiding the measurement that explains a failed attempt.

Manufacturing uses Factorio's cumulative per-force, per-surface production counters. An idle furnace may clear its active recipe, so multiplying its current recipe by its lifetime completion count can make past production disappear. A measured zero-energy or idle spare machine is an observed state, not a coverage gap. Script-inserted fixture supplies do not count as manufacturing; production, delivery and inventory balance remain separate checks.

Brief-derived assignments permit stock fluctuations of one second of target output, rounded up with a two-unit minimum (15 plates for the default). The former fixed two-item bound rejected ordinary conveyor phase differences even when every window manufactured and delivered over 1,000 plates against a 900-plate requirement. Both fresh manufacturing and delivery must still independently reach the full target in every window; residual tolerance stays one unit, and larger stock drawdown still fails. Explicit assignment tolerances remain unchanged. Historical failed results are never rescored into successes; new runs freeze the revised rule at admission.

The operator materialization payload limit is aligned at 4 MiB in TypeScript and Lua; ordinary gameplay remains limited to 64 KiB. Real-game guards cover large candidates, physical dual-lane delivery, absent manufacturing, fixture defaults and existing rollback behavior. A trial's diagnostic completion is separate from target success; a successful trial requires a completed, target-passing best iteration and exports its native blueprint alongside retained run evidence.

Scope is the blueprint workshop. Ordinary scenario resources, character rules and model policy are unchanged. This record does not claim a successful run; executed outcomes are recorded in the [implementation handoff](../IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md).
