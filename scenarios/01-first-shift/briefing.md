# First Shift

Build an automatic red-science factory delivering at least **30 packs per game minute for five consecutive minutes**.

You have a cleared 64×64 Nauvis site, a visible builder with normal movement and reach, and a finite construction kit. Labeled incoming yellow-belt terminals supply gears and copper plates at 60 items/minute each. A protected power connection supplies ample power; connect your own local poles. Route science automatically into the labeled draining collector.

The kit contains 12 assembling machines 1, 300 yellow belts, 60 inserters, 12 long-handed inserters, 12 underground-belt items, 6 splitters, 24 small electric poles and 8 wooden chests, all normal quality. You do not need to mine resources, clear terrain or craft construction equipment.

Use legal movement, placement, rotation, recipe selection, transfers and deconstruction within the site. The allowed production recipe is automation science. Fixture modifications and manual crafting are disabled. Observations expose the installed recipe and machine facts.

Request verification after the factory is running. Character mutations are cancelled before its baseline; only observations, messages and non-interacting walking remain available. After 600 settling ticks, five consecutive 3,600-tick windows must each contain at least 30 freshly machine-produced and automatically collected packs. The same period must show sufficient fresh terminal intake into the working science chain; old buffers and unrelated supply cannot substitute. Returning to construction aborts the attempt.

The initial limits are 30 game minutes and 90 wall minutes, including verification. Explicit pause freezes game time. Reset returns to the disarmed initial fixture with a new run identity, preserving the previous history. The operator selects either a team or solo roster using the same scenario rules.
