# Some Assembly Required

Build an automatic red-science factory delivering at least **30 packs per game minute for five consecutive minutes**, starting from plates.

The cleared 80×80 Nauvis site supplies iron plates at 120/minute and copper plates at 60/minute. Build connected iron-gear production, route gears and copper into automation-science assemblers, and deliver their output to the protected collector. Supplied power is protected; build local distribution. No production cell or gear stock is supplied.

Use only the finite normal-quality kit and legal character movement, placement, rotation, recipe selection, transfer and deconstruction. Automation science and iron gears are the allowed recipes. Verification requires fresh automatic intake of 300 iron plates and 150 copper plates, production and delivery of 150 connected gears, and five consecutive 30-pack science windows. Preloaded or character-supplied gears, disconnected matching production and shared-buffer drawdown do not pass.

Verification closes character mutations after an exact baseline, settles for 600 ticks, and samples five exact 3,600-tick windows. Reset returns to the fingerprinted disarmed fixture with a new run identity and retained prior history. Solo and team rosters receive the same fixture and rules.
