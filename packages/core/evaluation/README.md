# Verification engine

`VerificationEngine` evaluates one attempt using evaluator-owned measurements. `VerificationControl` is the separate operator-only Lua admission barrier. Neither is exposed through gameplay tools. Scenario adapters must retain events in evaluator-private storage, supply engine-derived measurements, poll independent wall limits even during pause, forward budget closure with `stop`, and forward lost coverage or uncertain edits with `invalidate`/`humanEdit`.

Sequence: configure the benchmark while disarmed; arm; request verification in the core; call the Lua admission barrier; obtain the calibrated factory sample at the acknowledged tick; acknowledge both in the core. Lua cancels active/queued orders and native crafting before capturing character inventory and receipts. The raw guard baseline explicitly has **no calibrated factory coverage** and cannot by itself satisfy a `Measurement`. If transport loses an acknowledgement, inspect the same attempt; never create a replacement baseline on assumption.

The scenario adapter must capture the admission tick, exact settling boundary (default 600 ticks), and each of five exact 3600-tick boundaries. Samples cover the preceding interval without gaps, including human/unknown edits, manual supply and artificial output. Repeated identical frozen-tick reads are allowed; missing boundaries are invalid, with no interpolation. Science and collector counters are cumulative and scoped to the connected production chain and draining collector. Interval contamination counts are zero at a newly reconciled baseline. Starting counter values are excluded.

Each required stage identifies a source, net transfer boundary, consuming chain and rich item identity. Production, forward/reverse transfers and consumption are cumulative. Both sides inventory containers, belts, inserter hands and in-process material. The evaluator checks separate conservation residuals and bounded drawdown, and requires the full fresh production/delivery/consumption minimum without subtracting tolerances. Missing coverage or unreconciled balances are invalid; proven inadequate output/flow is failed. Tolerance values and their calibration version are frozen in the manifest before admission.

One engine instance cannot restart an invalid, aborted or failed attempt. Repair must abort the core attempt before invoking the matching Lua repair acknowledgement; only then does Lua reopen mutations. New attempts require new identities and fresh samples. Science handcrafting and fixture protections remain active during repair. Lua invalidation keeps mutation admission closed; heartbeat loss or authority replacement invalidates its baseline. Ordinary pause freezes game ticks; wall-time limits still apply.

Events retain incremental samples/window results, a separate manifest/admission, and raw rejected evidence on invalidation. `report()` explicitly reconstructs a complete snapshot; routine sinks should retain the incremental events instead of requesting a full snapshot on every poll.

Phase 10 proves deterministic traces and a live guard sandbox. Phase 11 adds the [S1 adapter](../../factorio/src/first-shift.ts), exact engine-tick samples, a draining collector and live positive/bypass calibration; see the [current acceptance evidence](../../../docs/IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md). The shared engine still has no quantitative fuel measurement.
